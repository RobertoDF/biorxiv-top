Which neuroscience preprints are people reading?

Preprints ranked by reads per day: full-text views + PDF downloads in the period (abstract views are left out) ÷ days the preprint was online in it, across every tracked preprint, whenever it was posted. A preprint needs at least days online in the period to be ranked. bioRxiv reports usage per calendar month, so periods are whole months, and the current month counts up to the latest data. Pick a timeframe or a single month, then click a paper to see its rank over time.
